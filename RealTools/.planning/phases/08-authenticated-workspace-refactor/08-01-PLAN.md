<objective>
Refactor the authenticated app shell, sidebar, nav, and logout affordance into the approved light SaaS dashboard layout while preserving authentication and navigation behavior.
</objective>

<threat_model>
Security-sensitive behavior in this plan is the authenticated route guard and logout flow.

Threats:
- HIGH: Removing or weakening `supabase.auth.getUser()` or `redirect('/auth/login')` would expose authenticated pages.
- MEDIUM: Changing nav route prefixes could route brokers to wrong pages or break Deal Hub active state.
- MEDIUM: Changing logout flow could leave stale authenticated state in the browser.

Mitigations:
- Preserve `createSupabaseServerClient`, `supabase.auth.getUser()`, and `redirect('/auth/login')` in `app/(app)/layout.tsx`.
- Preserve `navItems` hrefs and `matchPrefixes` values in `components/sidebar-nav.tsx`.
- Preserve `createSupabaseBrowserClient`, `supabase.auth.signOut()`, `router.push('/auth/login')`, and `router.refresh()` in `components/logout-button.tsx`.
</threat_model>
